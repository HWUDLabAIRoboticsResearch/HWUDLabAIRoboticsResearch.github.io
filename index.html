Our strategic foundation

LAIRR operates at the intersection of world-class academic research and industrial application. We are part of the Global Research Institute (GRI) in AI and Robotics at Heriot-Watt University, leveraging this prestigious ecosystem to push the boundaries of cognitive intelligence.

Our research is physically and strategically anchored within the UK National Robotarium (UKNR). This unique partnership gives us access to unparalleled state-of-the-art testing facilities and high-performance computing — enabling us to translate complex theoretical models into real-world, industry-ready solutions.

Student project highlights

LiZIP

A lightweight, near-lossless compression framework for LiDAR point clouds, using a compact neural predictive-coding model to encode only sparse residuals. LiZIP outperforms industry-standard LASzip by 7.5–14.8% and Google Draco by 8.8–11.3% on the NuScenes and Argoverse datasets, easing the real-time processing and V2X bandwidth bottleneck in autonomous vehicles.

Student project

CANSURF

A vision system and open dataset for spotting small, reflective marine debris — aluminum cans on water — from an autonomous surface vehicle. Built from ∼7.3k annotated images (expanded to ∼57k via augmentation), it boosts YOLOv11 performance 12× over generic datasets; YOLOv11+ByteTrack gives the most stable multi-object tracking, while YOLOv11+SAHI maximizes far-field detection for single-can pickup missions.

Our strategic foundation

LAIRR operates at the intersection of world-class academic research and industrial application. We are part of the Global Research Institute (GRI) in AI and Robotics at Heriot-Watt University, leveraging this prestigious ecosystem to push the boundaries of cognitive intelligence.

Our research is physically and strategically anchored within the UK National Robotarium (UKNR). This unique partnership gives us access to unparalleled state-of-the-art testing facilities and high-performance computing — enabling us to translate complex theoretical models into real-world, industry-ready solutions.

Core technical expertise

06 AREAS

Robot Learning & Manipulation

Teaching robots to grasp, manipulate, and adapt through learned policies rather than hand-coded rules.

Navigation & Localisation

Enabling autonomous systems to understand where they are and move safely through unpredictable spaces.

Physical Human-Robot Interaction

Designing safe, intuitive collaboration between people and robots sharing the same physical space.

Robotics Vision

Perception systems that let robots see, interpret, and react to complex, cluttered environments.

Mechatronics, Sensing & Control

Integrating hardware, sensors, and control systems into cohesive, reliable robotic platforms.

Optimisation & Control

Formal, mathematically grounded methods for planning and controlling robot behaviour under uncertainty.

Active · Feb 2025 – Jan 2028

CogWaters

Engineering a cognitive architecture for Unmanned Surface Vehicles (USVs) that delivers scalable, intelligent debris removal. CogWaters serves as a key driver for industrial ESG solutions and advanced robotic autonomy.

GBOT

Completed

A robotic system for the detection and collection of debris on water surfaces, competing in autonomous and teleoperated tracks. Funded by Mubadala's Higher Education Student Competition, GBOT won third prize in the autonomous track.

May 2024 – Apr 2025 · Lead: Claudio Zito

R2PO

Active

Resilient Robot Perception & Operations — focused on robust perception for complex environments. By enabling machines to navigate uncertainty with high reliability, R2PO provides the foundational intelligence for safe, autonomous industrial operations.
